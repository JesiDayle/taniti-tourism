Travel Essentials

Currency

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen. Banks offer currency exchange services, and many businesses accept major credit cards.

Electricity

Power outlets use 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. English is less commonly spoken in rural areas, particularly among older residents.

Health & Safety

Medical Services

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. Many hospital employees speak multiple languages.

Personal Safety

Violent crime is very rare on Taniti. However, pickpocketing and other petty crimes have increased as tourism has grown. Visitors should keep personal belongings secure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What currency is used on Taniti?

The U.S. dollar is the official currency. Euros and yen are also accepted by many businesses.

Are credit cards accepted?

Many businesses accept major credit cards.

What is the legal drinking age?

The drinking age on Taniti is 18. Alcohol cannot be sold or served between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Are businesses open during holidays?

Taniti celebrates many national holidays. Some restaurants and tourist attractions may close during these holidays, so visitors should plan ahead.

How can I travel around the island?

Visitors can use buses, taxis, rental cars, bicycles, or explore walkable areas.

Are bicycle helmets required?

Yes. Bicycle helmets are required by law when riding on Taniti.
