Getting to Taniti

Most visitors arrive on Taniti by air. The island has a small airport that accommodates small jets and propeller planes.

Some visitors arrive by cruise ship. A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week.

The airport is being expanded to accommodate larger jets in the coming years.

Getting Around the Island

Public Buses

Public buses operate throughout Taniti City daily from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Private buses serve other parts of the island.

Taxis

Taxis are available within Taniti City for visitors who prefer direct transportation.

Rental Cars

Visitors can rent cars from a local rental agency near the airport.

Walking & Biking

Walking

Taniti City is relatively flat and easy to explore on foot.

Merriton Landing is another walkable area, with nearby entertainment and attractions.

Bicycle Rentals

Bicycles and helmets are available to rent from several vendors around the island. Helmets are required by law.

Transportation Information & Rental Options

Whether you prefer public transportation, driving, biking, or walking, Taniti offers several ways to explore the island.

Consider your accommodations and planned activities when choosing transportation.
